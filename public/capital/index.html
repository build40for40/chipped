The full leaderboard

Every chip and nail company we could price. The white tick on each bar is what the same $1,000 in the S&P 500 would be worth over the same years.

Chocolate chips beat microchips. Nail guns lost to the market. And if your nails are chipped, at least you didn't buy the polish, which turned $1,000 into $280.

How

Yahoo Finance adjusted closing prices, so dividends are reinvested, from March 1980 or the listing date to 13 November 2026. The S&P 500 is Robert Shiller's total return series over the same windows, topped up with SPY to today.

Survivors only

Only companies still trading have prices. Revlon, the classic nail polish brand, went bankrupt in 2022 and its shareholders were wiped out. US Steel was bought out in 2025. Both are missing, which flatters the list.

Hindsight

These companies were picked in 2026. In 1980 nobody knew steel would beat AMD. This is a pub fact, not investment advice.
